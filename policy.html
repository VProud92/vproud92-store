Privacy Policy

At VProud92, we respect your privacy and are committed to protecting any personal information you share with us when placing an order or browsing our website.

Information We Collect

When you place an order via our checkout system, we collect basic details necessary to fulfill your delivery, including your full name, phone number/WhatsApp, and delivery address.

Your information is solely used to process and deliver your Cash on Delivery (COD) orders and to communicate with you regarding your shipment via WhatsApp or phone call. We never share or sell your personal data to third parties.

Security

We take appropriate measures to ensure your data remains secure. Since orders are processed directly through WhatsApp, your communication with us remains private and secure.
